Sell an Inherited House in California

Probate sales, heir disagreements, and how to get cash without rushing a sale.

Inheriting a house in California often means waiting through probate while the mortgage, taxes, insurance, and upkeep keep running. If you are searching for how to sell an inherited house in California, here is what usually happens — and when an inheritance advance is a better fit than forcing a sale.

Can you sell a house during probate in California?

Often yes — but not always immediately. The personal representative (executor or administrator) typically needs court authority for a probate sale, and the process can include notices, appraisals, and confirmation hearings. That takes time. Heirs who are not the personal representative usually cannot sell the house on their own.

What slows down selling an inherited home

  • Waiting for letters of administration or probate to open
  • Multiple heirs who disagree on price, timing, or whether to sell at all
  • Title, liens, or creditor claims that must clear first
  • Court confirmation requirements on some probate sales
  • Repairs, occupancy, or tenant issues that scare off buyers

See also when heirs disagree about selling inherited property and how long California probate takes.

Selling vs. keeping the house

Selling turns the property into cash for all heirs (after debts and costs). Keeping it preserves the asset — but someone still has to carry the expenses until probate distributes title. Many heirs do not need to sell; they need bridge cash while the case runs.

When an inheritance advance makes more sense

An inheritance advance pays you a portion of your expected inheritance now and is settled from the estate later. There is no credit check and no monthly payment. You do not have to force a sale just to cover funeral costs, travel, taxes, or sibling buyout pressure.

Bottom line: If the plan is to sell eventually, a probate sale can work — but if you need cash now and want options, get an advance evaluation before you lock into a sale timeline.
